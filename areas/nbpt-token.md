# NBPT / NPETF Governance & Deployment Scorecard Standard

**Description:** Canonical NBPT/NPETF Governance & Deployment Scorecard Standard.

## Canonical Operational Status

**Status:** `HOLD [stated]`

This status is a canonical governance declaration. It is not independent evidence of deployment, legal clearance, active liquidity, market availability, or regulatory approval.

## Governance Preconditions

No token generation, liquidity-pool initialization, smart-contract deployment, treasury transfer, liquidity provisioning, or capital routing may proceed until all of the following are satisfied:

1. Required compliance gates are complete.
2. Jurisdiction-specific legal sign-offs are documented.
3. Applicable regulatory and licensing requirements are verified.
4. Human authorization thresholds are satisfied.
5. Required evidence is recorded in the canonical governance system.

## Artifact Handling

Pitch decks, tokenomics spreadsheets, pre-launch simulation reports, deployment PDFs, architectural specifications, and similar materials are classified as **Evidence Class 8 — User-Provided Reference Artifacts**.

These artifacts are unverified inputs and cannot independently establish:

- Active liquidity.
- Legal clearance.
- Production deployment.
- Regulatory approval.
- Smart-contract readiness.
- Treasury readiness.
- Capital-routing authority.

## Prohibited Autonomous Actions

The following actions are strictly prohibited without explicit, out-of-band human confirmation and completion of all applicable governance gates:

- Token minting or generation.
- Treasury transfers.
- Smart-contract deployment.
- Liquidity-pool initialization.
- Liquidity provisioning.
- Capital routing.
- Any equivalent production financial action.

Agentic recommendations, generated plans, simulations, and internal reasoning cannot substitute for human authorization.

## Evidence Threshold Rule

Evidence Class 8 — User-Provided Reference Artifacts and Evidence Class 9 — Simulated / Synthetic Data Outputs can never independently satisfy production deployment, active-liquidity, legal-clearance, or operational-readiness thresholds.

## Change-Control Rule

Future revisions must preserve prior history and use merge-and-append change control rather than destructive overwrite. Any status change from `HOLD [stated]` requires named human approval, dated evidence, jurisdictional review where applicable, and a recorded authorization decision.
