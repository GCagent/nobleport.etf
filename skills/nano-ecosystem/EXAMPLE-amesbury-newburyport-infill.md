# Worked example — Amesbury / Newburyport 6-unit infill + 2 ADUs

**Status:** hypothetical workflow demonstration. Not a live parcel, zoning,
valuation, or acquisition determination.

**Program:** 6-unit infill + 2 ADUs (8 completed units)
**Market:** Amesbury / Newburyport
**Site id:** SITE-001
**Overall state:** `STAGED — DUE DILIGENCE REQUIRED`

This is the golden narrative the nano-ecosystem skills (**16–25**) and the
`nano_infill_chain` workflow must reproduce. Swap in verified parcel, zoning,
and market evidence before treating any figure as live.

## 1. Hyperlocal Site Selector → STAGED

Stephanie identifies a hypothetical underutilized parcel and creates the
acquisition screen: zoning/use compatibility, lot geometry, utilities, access,
flood/environmental exposure, comparable development, and permitting complexity.

**Output:** SITE-001 advances to feasibility, subject to verified parcel and
municipal data.

**Gate:** Stephanie does not authorize acquisition. The model advances only
for deeper due diligence.

## 3. Entitlement → Design → Construction

The Entitlement Navigator generates a verification matrix covering dimensional
requirements, density, parking, ADU eligibility, utilities, fire access,
stormwater, conservation/flood issues, and required municipal approvals.

Nothing gets labeled “permitted” until source evidence supports it.

Once entitlement assumptions survive review, Generative Design Coordinator
develops three concepts — maximum yield, lower-cost construction, and
balanced yield/community fit.

The selected concept then feeds Construction Sequence Optimizer:

Preconstruction → sitework → foundation → framing/dry-in → MEP rough →
inspections → insulation → drywall → finishes → exterior/site completion →
CO/closeout.

Supplier availability, inspection dependencies, weather exposure and long-lead
items become critical-path inputs.

## 4. Capital and risk layer

The Construction Loan Draw Manager continuously reconciles contract budget,
approved changes, completed work, invoices, lien-waiver status and inspection
evidence.

Stephanie can assemble:

```
Draw #04 — DRAFT / HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED
Requested: $287,500
Evidence completeness: 94%
Exception: electrical change order lacks final approval.
Action: HOLD affected amount.
```

The Risk & Resilience Stress Tester simultaneously runs cost escalation,
schedule delay, rate, absorption and permitting scenarios instead of relying
on the base case alone.

## 5. Operations after completion

After closeout, the asset moves into Smart Property Management Copilot for
maintenance, vendor coordination, operating-cost tracking and recurring-problem
detection.

The Ecosystem Orchestrator then connects the entire record:

Parcel → entitlement → drawings → estimate → subcontractors → schedule →
inspections → draws → closeout → tenants → maintenance → asset performance.

That is the real value of the architecture: one project becomes a reusable
evidence graph instead of disappearing into PDFs, emails, spreadsheets and
people’s heads.
