# Governed AI operating stack

## Purpose
NVIDIA and its infrastructure partners supply processing capacity.
Stephanie.ai turns that capacity into construction and business results.
The connection is **PROPOSED**. It is not deployed.

## When to use
- Rubin, Vera Rubin, Kyber, 800 VDC, power, or cooling claims.
- CIO-AI, NeMo, CUDAOrchestrator, AvatarGPU, AuditBeacon, or IPFS.
- A proposal to spend on compute, or to prove Stephanie on a closeout bundle.

## When NOT to use
- Authorizing a provider invoice, a contract, or a filing.
- Pricing NBPT or KUZO from an inference-cost slide.
- Treating ERC-3643 as a security certification or legal approval.
- Calling 236 High Road’s halted 8-unit scheme a closeout pilot.

## Milestones — not one rollout
| Milestone | When | Tag |

Do not collapse these into one shipment. NVIDIA efficiency targets are not a measured NoblePort saving. Savings depend on provider price and workload.

## Layers
1. Rubin, power, and cooling — capacity only. PROPOSED connection.
2. Stephanie directs the work. CIO-AI evaluates providers, integrations, reliability, and cost. PROPOSED. Neither approves money.
3. Stephanie Gateway / MCP — access, tool permissions, spending limits, evidence. A Rubin-class spend is not authorized.
4. Construction applications — estimates, permit-package checks, coordination, closeout bundles. A result counts when a person accepts it.
5. Evidence storage — source documents, approvals, receipts, audit records. Encrypt sensitive content. IPFS needs pinning and retention; a hash is not private and not durable by itself.
6. Optional tokenization — separately governed, only after the business and legal structure is approved. Out of the compute pilot.

## Named, not verified live
An internal audit document names NVIDIA NeMo, CUDAOrchestrator, AvatarGPU, AuditBeacon, and IPFS. Historical design connection. **UNVERIFIED** as a live integration.

## Distinctions
- AI tokens and NBPT/KUZO tokens are different. Lower inference cost does not establish token value.
- ERC-3643 governs permissioned security tokens. It is not a general security certification.
- IPFS needs encryption plus maintained storage and pinning.

## Release
Prove it on one real job closeout bundle. Measure preparation time, corrections, and cost per accepted result. Stephanie reviews first. A person approves business, money, contract, and professional decisions before execution. The pilot is not open.
